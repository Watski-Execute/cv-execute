# Opportunity Targeting

A good CV system should distinguish between:

## Broad opportunity discovery

Exploring multiple plausible organizational entry points.

and:

## Broad public positioning

Presenting many unrelated professional identities simultaneously.

The first can be useful.

The second usually reduces clarity.

## Core rule

> **Broad search. Narrow shot. Deep evidence behind it.**

The evidence bank is intentionally broad.

Each vacancy gets its own evidence matrix.

Each CV should present the smallest coherent professional identity that truthfully explains why the candidate can do that job.

## Fit classes

### DIRECT
The underlying responsibilities are strongly evidenced already.

### STRETCH
The candidate has strong adjacent evidence and the missing tools/domain knowledge are realistically learnable.

### BRIDGE
The role may be useful temporarily but is weaker against long-term goals or constraints.

### NO-GO
The role conflicts with hard constraints or requires genuinely senior/professionally gated competence the evidence does not support.

## Important distinction

A tool gap can sometimes be learned quickly.

A judgment gap cannot be hand-waved away.

Examples of the latter may include senior engineering, regulated professional work, advanced security, legal/accounting practice, or any role where years of domain judgment are the actual qualification.

## Tailoring

Do not rewrite the candidate's history for each role.

Keep evidence stable.

Change selection, hierarchy and vocabulary.
