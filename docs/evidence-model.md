# Evidence Model

The evidence bank is the foundation of CV Execute.

A CV is treated as a **selection and presentation layer**, not the source of truth.

## Recommended evidence entry

```markdown
## Customer troubleshooting

Status: VERIFIED

Sources:
- field service role
- repair cases
- independent client work

Supports:
- troubleshooting
- issue ownership
- customer communication
- root-cause thinking

Does not establish:
- enterprise SaaS support tenure
- Zendesk production experience
- CRM administration
```

## Why boundaries matter

LLMs are good at semantic translation.

They are also capable of gradually exaggerating a weak claim through repeated rewriting.

Explicit evidence boundaries help prevent:

> "worked with customer information"

from becoming:

> "5+ years of enterprise CRM expertise"

## Evidence strength

### VERIFIED
Direct fact.

### SUPPORTED
Reasonable professional interpretation of verified facts.

### ADJACENT
Useful transferable experience, but not identical to the employer requirement.

### UNVERIFIED
Candidate confirmation required.

### GAP
No present evidence.

### DO NOT CLAIM
Should not be stated as fact.

## Quantification

Use numbers when they are known and useful.

Never invent numbers to make a bullet look stronger.

Unquantified evidence is better than fabricated precision.
